The club

Class ends.
The evening doesn't have to.

Every member is invited to our private WhatsApp community. Each week I propose one thing to do together — a gallery opening, a movie night, a long dinner, a milonga for the brave. And members propose their own: a run club for tango feet, a Sunday museum walk, a table nobody wants to leave.

Questions

Fair things to ask before you join

I can barely walk in heels. Is this for me?
Honestly — that's exactly who this is for. Comfortable, beautiful walking in heels is a skill, and it's the first one we train. You'll be surprised what eight weeks does.
I've never danced. And what if I have?
One group, all levels — every exercise scales to you. Beginners get the foundations; experienced dancers get complexity, variations and sharper corrections. If your level turns out mismatched after the first class, we adjust — free.
I have no partner. Can I join?
Yes — everything we do is solo technique and choreo. Partner work is not required.
What are the terms of each pass?
Drop-in: any single session, reschedule free up to 12 hours before. 4-Class Pass: any four sessions within the module. Full Module: eight classes over eight weeks; one missed class is transferable to a friend.
What's the refund policy?
Full refund any time before the module starts — no questions asked, deposits included. If I cancel or postpone for any reason, you receive 100% back, always. After the module starts, places are transferable to a friend or convertible to credit.
What should I wear?
If you're new to dancing in heels, wear whatever you're comfortable in — low, stable heels or flat practice shoes are perfect for the first weeks. We'll build up together.
